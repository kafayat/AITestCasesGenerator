Sydney Water Bill Payment - Sample Test Cases

Test Case ID: SW-TC001
Title: Successful bill payment with valid account and card details
Priority: High
Type: Positive
Preconditions: Customer has a valid Sydney Water account and outstanding bill.
Test Data: Valid account number, outstanding amount, valid card details.
Steps:
1. Open Sydney Water bill payment page.
2. Enter valid account number.
3. View outstanding amount.
4. Select card payment.
5. Enter valid card details.
6. Submit payment.
Expected Result: Payment is successful, receipt number is generated, and confirmation is displayed.
Requirement ID: REQ-SW001

Test Case ID: SW-TC002
Title: Payment fails with invalid card details
Priority: High
Type: Negative
Preconditions: Customer has a valid Sydney Water account and outstanding bill.
Test Data: Valid account number, invalid card details.
Steps:
1. Open Sydney Water bill payment page.
2. Enter valid account number.
3. Select card payment.
4. Enter invalid card details.
5. Submit payment.
Expected Result: Payment is rejected and the bill remains unpaid.
Requirement ID: REQ-SW001

Test Case ID: SW-TC003
Title: Account number validation for invalid account
Priority: High
Type: Validation
Preconditions: Customer is on bill payment page.
Test Data: Invalid account number.
Steps:
1. Open Sydney Water bill payment page.
2. Enter invalid account number.
3. Submit or continue.
Expected Result: System displays validation error and does not show bill amount.
Requirement ID: REQ-SW001

Test Case ID: SW-TC004
Title: Prevent duplicate payment after successful payment
Priority: Medium
Type: Business Rule
Preconditions: Customer has already paid the bill successfully.
Test Data: Same account number and same bill reference.
Steps:
1. Open payment page again.
2. Enter same account number.
3. Attempt to pay the same bill again.
Expected Result: System prevents duplicate payment or warns customer that the bill has already been paid.
Requirement ID: REQ-SW001
