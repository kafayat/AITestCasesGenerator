REQ-SW001: Sydney Water Customer Bill Payment

As a Sydney Water customer,
I want to pay my water bill online,
So that I can complete my payment without visiting a service centre.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Customer can enter a valid Sydney Water account number.
- Customer can view the outstanding bill amount before payment.
- Customer can select a payment method such as card or bank payment.
- Customer must provide valid payment details.
- Payment must be processed securely.
- Payment confirmation must be displayed after successful payment.
- Receipt number must be generated after successful payment.
- Confirmation email or SMS should be sent to the customer.
- If payment fails, the bill should remain unpaid.
- If payment fails, the customer should see a clear error message.
- Duplicate payment should be prevented where possible.
- Customer should be able to return to the account dashboard after payment.
