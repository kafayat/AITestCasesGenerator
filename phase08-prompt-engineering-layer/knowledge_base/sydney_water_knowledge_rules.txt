Sydney Water Style Business Rules and QA Knowledge

Billing and Payment Rules:
- Customer must provide a valid account number before viewing bill details.
- Outstanding bill amount must be shown before customer submits payment.
- Payment must only be confirmed after successful payment processing.
- Failed payment must not mark the bill as paid.
- Duplicate payments should be prevented where possible.
- Receipt number must be generated for successful payment.
- Confirmation email or SMS should be sent after successful payment.
- Payment error messages should be clear but should not expose sensitive banking details.

Testing Standards:
- Include positive, negative, validation, boundary, and security test scenarios.
- Payment test cases must verify receipt generation and payment status update.
- Failed payment test cases must verify no account balance update occurs.
- Sensitive payment details must not be logged or displayed.
- Test cases must include clear preconditions, test data, steps, and expected results.
